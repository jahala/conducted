# Conductor success handlers: what each action does after it succeeds

Scope: every mutation or action a user triggers in Conductor 0.92.1, and what its success path does beyond the call itself: navigation, dialogs, toasts, focus, selections and store changes. Then the same action in Conducted, read from `apps/desktop/src`.

Evidence comes from the extracted frontend (`main-BE1h_1MP.js` and `renderApp-DeUrsznk.js`). Offsets follow the research convention: `m@N` is a character offset in `main-BE1h_1MP.js`, `r@N` is one in `renderApp-DeUrsznk.js`. Re-find any site with `python3 -I scripts/conductor-re/ctx.py <file> <regex>`.

## The class of miss

The owner found one instance: adding a project in Conductor creates and opens the project's first workspace (`r@1713146`), and our research never recorded that side effect. The sweep below finds the rest of the class: a successful call whose visible result is a navigation, a dialog, a toast or a selection that we did not rebuild because the research described only the call.

Eleven rows are outright misses and two are partial. The misses are first in the table, ordered by how central the action is: the three project-add paths first (first run), then daily workspace, chat and pull-request work.

## The gap this sweep found

The same rule was broken in several handlers: a successful call owes the user a place to look (a new workspace and its route after adding a project, Home after archiving, the next chat after closing) or a way to continue (Unarchive after a merged PR, View logs after a failed setup, Delete permanently after a corrupt archive). Conductor puts those in the mutation's success path, where a later refactor cannot drop them. Ours put the call in the service and left the success path empty.

The three project-add paths are one bug, not three. Conductor funnels local add, clone and quick start through `createRepo` (`r@1713146`), so the first-workspace creation and navigation live in one place. Ours has three separate components, and none of them knows that adding a project should open it. A fix belongs in one helper that all three call, or in the create-project action itself, not in three components.

Two more findings are not success handlers but were found while tracing them, and they are user-visible:

- Closing a chat in ours does not stop its agent (row 6). The chat disappears and the agent keeps working; Conductor stops it and asks first.
- Restoring a workspace in ours does not rerun its setup script (row 8), so a restored workspace can look ready with no dependencies installed.

## What the research lacked

These facts were not in `services-map.md` or `ui-map.md`, and are recorded here for the first time:

1. `createRepo`'s onSuccess creates the first workspace and navigates (`r@1714329`). This is the original bug.
2. The add-local path opens the create-private-repo dialog for non-git folders and for git folders without an origin (`r@1717588`).
3. Quick start creates its first workspace explicitly, after the repo step (`r@2034890`), and its success toast carries an "Open in GitHub" action.
4. The archive confirmation dialog's exact states and wording, including the open-PR checklist (`r@11399984`, `r@11401303`).
5. The archive-on-merge toasts and their Open PR / Unarchive actions, and the stack exception (`m@733372`).
6. Unarchive reruns the setup script when the restore needs it (`m@3758333`).
7. Closing a chat stops its agent and sets the next active session (`m@3635568`, `m@3636298`).
8. A successful Claude sign-in stops idle sessions and says so (`r@9511478`).

## Method

- Read every `onSuccess` (191 sites across the two bundles) and every `mutateAsync`/`.mutate` call near a user action, then followed the named handlers to their definitions.
- Checked each named action class from the brief: projects, workspaces, chats, messages, pull requests, commits and pushes, settings saves and sign-ins.
- For each, read our equivalent under `apps/desktop/src` and the caller, not just the service, because the visible side effects live in the caller.
- No product code was changed. This file is the evidence for the lead's next builds.
